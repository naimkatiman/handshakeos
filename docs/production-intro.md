HandshakeOS production intro
31 October 2026

Aim for roughly 45 seconds before the live walkthrough. Rehearse the timing.
The founder and partner act out the fictional LintasExpress sample.

Hook
Action: The founder holds up a business card and checks their phone.
Founder: “We shook hands last night. So why is the deal still standing still?”

Stakes
Action: The partner walks away. The founder starts a follow-up, then leaves it in drafts.
Founder: “Sarah asked about a pilot. I promised to follow up. Next week, this could be just another conversation buried in my phone.”

Turn
Action: The founder opens HandshakeOS and starts the sample deal.
Founder: “This time, I’m turning the conversation into a next step.”

Scene
Action: Cut from the quiet event table to the screen. Show the action plan, then Sarah’s reply in the guide.
Founder: “The room is quiet. My phone lights up. Sarah says the pilot is live on ten vans.”
Action: Select Verify Sarah's reply. Let the quoted evidence appear. Select Confirm: POC launched.
Founder: “Her words are the evidence. I check them. I confirm the step.”

Shatter
Action: Hold on the confirmed milestone. Pause before the last sentence.
Founder: “A handshake starts it. Evidence moves it.”

Hand the experience over
Action: Show the landing page’s bottom section with its QR code and Start the sample deal button.
Founder: “Scan this and try the same conversation. Then send the rumour and see what happens.”

Walkthrough cues
1. Start the sample deal opens /capture?sample=en&run=1.

3. Select Verify Sarah's reply, review the evidence, then Confirm: POC launched.
4. Select Send the rumour, then Reject it.
